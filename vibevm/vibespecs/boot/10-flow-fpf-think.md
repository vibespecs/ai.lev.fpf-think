# fpf-think — Foundational Thinking DPF Suite

5 DPF packages, 66 patterns. Choose the DPF by your working question, then open its Table of Contents (`spec://ai.lev/<package>/pub/TOC#root`). Suite README: spec://ai.lev/fpf-think/pub/README#root; Suite Reference with cases that combine DPFs: spec://ai.lev/fpf-think/pub/REFERENCE#root.

Which DPF for what:

- Split or change a calculation while keeping its answer meaningful -> `fpf-think-math` (20 patterns)
- Decide a question without reconstructing every hidden detail -> `fpf-think-mmp` (14 patterns)
- Develop a physical prediction and decide which observation matters -> `fpf-think-phy` (10 patterns)
- Construct an algorithm and adapt it when the required answer changes -> `fpf-think-cmp` (14 patterns)
- Carry a changed instruction through several expressions -> `fpf-think-not` (8 patterns)
